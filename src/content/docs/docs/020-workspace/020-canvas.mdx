# Canvas

The Canvas is the central area of the workspace where you create, edit, and preview your artwork and animations. Everything you draw or animate exists within the canvas boundaries.

## What the Canvas Shows

The canvas displays your SVG document at the current zoom level. A ruler along the top and left edges helps with measurement and placement. Optionally, a grid overlay and canvas outline can be shown to assist with alignment.

Objects placed outside the canvas boundary are still part of the document but can be hidden by enabling **Clip Canvas Content**.

## Configuring the Canvas

Display options — ruler, grid, outline, background, and clipping — are available in the [Canvas Display Options](../../docs/canvas-display-options) page. Canvas size and aspect ratio are set in the [Properties Panel](./properties-panel) when the `<svg/>` root is selected, or interactively using the Page Tool. See [Change Canvas Size](../../docs/change-canvas-size).

## Navigating the Canvas

Pan and zoom controls let you move around and adjust the magnification of the canvas view. See [Pan](../../docs/pan) and [Zoom](../../docs/zoom).
