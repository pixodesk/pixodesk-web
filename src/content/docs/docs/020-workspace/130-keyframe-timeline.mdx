# Keyframe Timeline

The Keyframe Editor is the central area of the [Timeline Panel](./timeline-panel). It provides a visual, time-based view of all keyframes in the animation.

## Time Ruler

The ruler along the top of the keyframe area shows time in seconds. The current playhead position is indicated by a vertical line. Drag the playhead to scrub through the animation and preview it at any point in time.

## Keyframes

Each animated property has a track in the keyframe area. Keyframes appear as diamond-shaped markers on the track at the time positions where property values are defined.

- **Select a keyframe** — click it; hold `Ctrl/Cmd` to add to the selection
- **Move a keyframe** — drag it left or right to shift its time position
- **Select multiple** — drag a selection rectangle across keyframes

## Easing Mini Charts

Between any two keyframes, the segment displays a **mini chart** — a small visual representation of the easing function applied to that transition. The mini chart gives an at-a-glance preview of the curve shape (ease-in, ease-out, bounce, etc.) directly in the timeline without opening the Chart Panel.

**Selecting and editing a segment's easing:**

Click on the segment between two keyframes to select it. This opens the [Easing Panel](./easing-charts#easing-panel) and also updates the [Chart Panel](./easing-charts) on the right of the Timeline Panel to show the curve for that specific segment.

## Animation Range

A highlighted region on the ruler indicates the active animation range (the total duration of the animation). Keyframes outside this range are not included in playback.

## Inner Loops

Selected keyframes can be marked as part of an **Inner Loop** — a sub-range that loops independently within the main animation. Use **Add Inner Loop to Selected Keyframes** in the toolbar or the More (⋮) menu to apply one.

## Keyframe Operations (More Menu)

The **More (⋮)** menu in the playback toolbar provides additional operations on selected keyframes:

- **Select All Keyframes** — selects every keyframe in the timeline
- **Deselect Keyframes** — clears the selection
- **Add Inner Loop to Selected Keyframes**
- **Remove Inner Loop from Selected Keyframes**
- **Flip Selected Keyframes** — reverses the order of selected keyframes in time
