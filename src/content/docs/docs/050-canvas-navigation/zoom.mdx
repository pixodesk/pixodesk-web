# Zoom

Zoom controls adjust the magnification of the canvas view. They do not affect the document or the final output — only how the canvas appears on screen.

## How to Zoom

- **Ctrl/Cmd + scroll** — zoom in and out at the cursor position
- **`+` / `-`** keys — zoom in or out by one step
- **Zoom dropdown** in the toolbar — access named zoom levels and fit options

## Zoom Options

- **Zoom In** — increase magnification one step
- **Zoom Out** — decrease magnification one step
- **Zoom 100%** — reset to actual size (1:1 pixel ratio)
- **Zoom to Fit** — fit all document content within the visible window
- **Zoom to Page** — fit the canvas boundary within the visible window
- **Zoom to Selection** — zoom in to focus on the currently selected objects

## Timeline Zoom

To adjust the horizontal zoom of the timeline view (how many frames are visible), use the **Zoom** control in the [Timeline Panel](../../docs/timeline-layers#zoom). The **Zoom Timeline to Frame Range** option in the Zoom dropdown also fits the timeline view to the full animation duration.
